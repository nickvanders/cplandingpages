ADHD Assessment & Coaching Program (Adults, Melbourne)

ADHD is a lifelong neurodevelopmental condition that can affect attention, impulse control, energy regulation, executive functioning and emotional regulation. It presents differently for everyone — inattention, hyperactivity, impulsivity, difficulties organising or regulating emotions may all be part of the picture.

Why get assessed

There remains a lot of misinformation and stigma around ADHD. This often leads to missed diagnoses and confusion. If you or someone you care about demonstrates some of the common signs of ADHD, a comprehensive assessment is a vital first step toward clarity and improved wellbeing.

How the program works

Accurate diagnosis — We begin with an in-person assessment by a qualified ADHD psychologist. We use evidence-based clinical interviews and psychometric instruments in a holistic approach that considers the individual’s brain, body and social environment.

Personalised treatment and intervention — If the assessment supports an ADHD diagnosis, your psychologist will design a treatment plan tailored to your lifestyle, needs and goals. This may include psycho-education, behavioural strategies, support for executive functioning, emotional regulation, daily life management and other interventions as needed.

Our approach

Our psychologists integrate evidence-based assessment with a neuroscience-informed perspective. We combine rigorous clinical methods with a holistic understanding of mind, body and environment. Our care is always tailored to each individual — recognising that no two people’s experiences are the same. We emphasise collaboration, supporting clients with education, validation, and practical tools rather than relying solely on prescriptive or one-size-fits-all methods.
